Bringing a puppy home is an exciting experience. It is also the beginning of an important learning journey.

## Start with the basics

Every puppy benefits from a consistent routine, positive reinforcement and patience.

## Teach essential commands

Start with simple commands such as:

- Sit
- Stay
- Come
- Leave it

Keep training sessions short and reward good behaviour.

## Build a consistent routine

Consistency helps puppies understand what is expected of them. Establish regular feeding, walking, play and rest schedules.

## Be patient and positive

Every dog learns at a different pace. Celebrate small improvements and make training an enjoyable experience.

## Final thoughts

Good training is about building trust and communication between you and your dog.
